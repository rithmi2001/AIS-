Child Protection

Our commitment to the safety, welfare and wellbeing of every student.

The school is committed to providing a safe and secure environment to all students.

We have a zero-tolerance policy on bullying which is communicated very clearly to all students. TASLAH which stands for "Take a Stand and Lend a Hand" is a student movement designed to create a counter culture that facilitates inclusion and acceptance in all spheres of school life.

Our tutorial and coaching staff have all subscribed to a policy document upholding high standards of professional conduct when dealing with our students. Our teachers are trained to look out for possible signs of distress, trauma and abuse in children in order to facilitate intervention if necessary. The entire community upholds the value of mutual respect and are committed to providing a welcoming school environment, safeguarding is not just about protecting students from harm but also linked to welfare and the general wellbeing which we extend to all our children.
