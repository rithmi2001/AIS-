Z Club of AIS Holds Annual General Meeting 2024/25 and Inducts New Committee for 2025/26'

Blog
📅 November 13, 2025 💬 Blog

The Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Z Club of AIS, was held on 31st October Friday.
The Meeting consisted of the reports of the past board for 2024/25, the installation of 47 new club members, and the induction fo the new committee for 2025/2026.

The event was graced by the Principal of AIS, all sectional heads, and our distinguished guests - the Z club sponsor club President Zontian Shevanthi Rajanathan, Z Club Coordinator Zontian Manthri Perera, and Zontian Dinali Wajeeswera.
The chief guest at the event was ma'am Shahela Sabil.

The new members who were installed took the Z Club oath, pledging their allegiance to the Club.

The induction of the new committee and the installation of the new Club members for the year 2025/26 was a glamorous event with the AIS oriental dancers and Senior Choir of AIS gracing the stage for entertainment with beautiful performances.

Thank you to Miss Tharaka Mendis, our guiding light who helped coordinate the event, and to our entire new committee for 2025/26, for putting in the work to pull off the feat.
